Privacy Policy — Sort ZEN

Last updated: June 19, 2026 · App: Sort ZEN (com.sortzen.app)

Sort ZEN ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Sort ZEN mobile application (the "Service"). This page explains what data is handled when you use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) ourselves. All game progress, coins, and settings are stored locally on your device. No account or login is required to play.

We use the following third-party services, which may collect limited information:

  • Google AdMob — serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID and ad-performance data to show personalized or non-personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes optional subscription and one-time "remove ads" purchases. Payment details are handled securely by Google; we only receive the purchase status.

2. Diagnostics & Crash Logs

If the Service crashes or errors, anonymous diagnostic and crash data may be collected to improve stability. This data does not contain personal information.

3. Data Security

All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS.

4. Children's Privacy

The Service is rated for general audiences but is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will remove it.

5. Your Choices

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact Us
